# Create: Industrial Pressure

**Industrial-grade fluid infrastructure for Create.** Copper pipes are fine for a starter base — but when you're moving lava across a mega-factory or feeding a wall of boilers, you need *pressure*. Industrial Pressure adds high pressure pipes and a full ladder of pumps that push fluids **further, faster, and harder** than anything in vanilla Create.

And pressure is just the beginning. This addon is built to grow into a complete industrial-fluids toolkit — see the [Roadmap](#roadmap) below.

---

## High Pressure Pipes

Drop-in upgrades for Create's copper pipes. They connect, render, and join Create's fluid network exactly like the originals — they just don't buckle under serious pressure.

- **Built to take it** — immune to bursting, no matter how hard you push.
- **See-through variant** — wrench a pipe to expose the flow inside, just like vanilla glass pipes.
- **Familiar feel** — same shapes, connections, and wrench behavior you already know.

## Tiered Pumps

The pump is where the power comes from. Each tier reaches further and moves dramatically more fluid — at a matching Stress cost. Reach scales with rotation speed, so spin them up to hit full range.

| Pump | Max Reach | Throughput |
|------|-----------|------------|
| High Pressure Pump | 64 blocks | 4× |
| High Pressure Pump II | 128 blocks | 8× |
| High Pressure Pump III | 256 blocks | 16× |
| **Netherite Pressure Pump** | 512 blocks | 32× |
| **Advanced Netherite Pump** | 1024 blocks | 64× |

Higher pressure means tanks, boilers, and machines fill and drain **much** faster — and a pump sitting in water pulls far more per tick.

## Pipes Burst Under Pressure

Hook a high pressure pump up to flimsy copper pipes and they'll **crack, then burst** at the weak points — spilling their contents. Want to run the big pumps? Build your network out of pipes that can handle it. It's a real engineering constraint, not just a number.

## Netherite Endgame

A top-tier material line for late-game factories: the **Netherite Pressure Pipe** (and its glass variant) plus two netherite pump tiers reaching all the way to **1024 blocks** at **64×** throughput. Copper tiers craft on the table as a simple upgrade path; the netherite tier is assembled in the **Mechanical Crafter** with proper multi-block patterns.

## Built for Big Networks

Massive pipe systems shouldn't tank your TPS. The pressure and bursting logic is event-driven, throttled, and bounded — so even sprawling factories stay smooth.

## Roadmap

"Industrial Pressure" is meant to outgrow its own name. Planned and under consideration:

- **Steam pipes & lines** — dedicated infrastructure for moving steam around big power setups.
- **Steam-powered pistons** — pistons driven directly by piped steam for high-force industrial contraptions.
- **More pressure-rated machinery** — valves, regulators, and fittings tuned for high-flow builds.
- **Further material tiers** and quality-of-life tweaks driven by your feedback.

Got an idea? Suggestions are very welcome.
